# Futuregames Customer Growth Hackathon Oct 2026

### Brief and prototypes in ./anitya-docs/

## Hackathon Challenge Description
Futuregames 13 & 15 October 2026 | Online
A real company challenge. A real team. A real opportunity to be seen.
In January 2027, many of you will begin your LIA period.
The current market is challenging, and we believe that Futuregames has a responsibility
not only to prepare you with the right knowledge and skills, but also to create
opportunities where you can show companies what you are capable of before LIA
begins.
That is why we are organising the Futuregames Customer Growth Hackathon.
During the hackathon, students from Experience Designer and Frontend Developer
will work together in cross-functional teams on a real business challenge submitted by
a participating company.
The goal is simple:
Use what you have learned to create something that could genuinely help a
company grow.
What will you work on?
Each participating company will submit a real challenge related to customer growth.
This could involve areas such as:
• Customer acquisition
• Customer experience
• Digital presence
• Conversion
• Engagement
• Customer journeys
• Digital products or services
• New ways of reaching or supporting customers
There will be a maximum of 4 participating companies and approximately 10 student
teams, meaning that two teams may work independently on the same company
challenge.
This means the companies receive different perspectives and approaches, while you
get the opportunity to work on a challenge that comes directly from the market.
Why are we doing this?
The hackathon is designed to give you something that a normal classroom assignment
cannot fully provide:
A real company. A real problem. A real audience.
You will have the opportunity to:
• Work across disciplines with Experience Design and Frontend Development
• Apply your skills to a real business challenge
• Practice rapid problem solving and decision-making
• Develop concepts and prototypes under time pressure
• Present your work directly to a company
• Receive feedback from industry representatives
• Create work that can become part of your professional portfolio
• Increase your visibility ahead of LIA
The overall purpose is to help you collaborate across areas of expertise, apply your
knowledge in a real case, and practice presentation, problem solving and teamwork.
How the hackathon works

1. Kick-off & Team Start
   Tuesday 13 October | 09:00-10:30 | Online
   All Experience Designer and Frontend Developer students participating in the
   hackathon will meet for a common kick-off.
   During the session we will:
   • Introduce the hackathon and its objectives
   • Present the participating companies
   • Present the company challenges
   • Introduce the expected deliverables
   • Announce the teams
   • Assign each team its company case
   The kick-off brings together students from EXD Online 25 and FED25 and marks the
   official start of the challenge.
   After the kick-off, your team starts working.
2. The Hackathon Sprint
   Your team will have one intensive working day to:
   Understand the challenge → explore opportunities → develop a solution → build
   something visible → prepare your pitch
   The work may include:
   • Research and problem framing
   • Customer or user perspectives
   • Ideation
   • Customer journeys or service concepts
   • UX/UI concepts
   • Technical solutions
   • Prototypes
   • Landing pages or digital experiences
   • Recommendations for the company
   The emphasis is not on creating a completely finished commercial product.
   The goal is to create a clear, convincing and well-developed solution concept that
   demonstrates how your combined skills can create value for the company.
   OBS! Use AI for the thing you already know how to do and to win time. Do not use AI to
   have the job done without understanding the whys and hows behind.
   Your deliverables
   Each team must prepare two things.
3. A visible digital solution and a presentation in PDF format
   Your work must be accessible through a link that can be shared with the company.
   Depending on your solution, this could include:
   • A working prototype
   • A landing page
   • A digital experience
   • A visual case presentation
   • A concept demonstration
   • Supporting documentation
   Technical production should, where appropriate, be published through platforms such
   as GitHub or Vercel, so that the company can access and review the result after the
   hackathon.
   Other suitable platforms may also be used depending on the nature of your solution.
   The important principle is:
   The company should be able to open one link and clearly understand what your
   team has created.
4. A short company pitch
   Thursday 15 October | 10:00-12:00 | Online
   Each team will present its solution during the final pitch session. The pitch will also be
   supported by the presentation in PDF mentioned above. The presentation will be shared
   with the company and will also be used as promotion material on the Futuregames
   website.
   You will have:
   Maximum 10 minutes for your presentation
   followed by approximately:
   2 minutes of direct feedback from the company, when a company representative is
   present.
   Your pitch should clearly explain:
   The challenge → your insight → your solution → how it creates value
   Do not only show what you created.
   Explain why your solution makes sense.
   Your work can become visible beyond the hackathon
   One important part of this initiative is to give you greater professional visibility before
   LIA.
   Selected team cases will be presented through Futuregames' digital channels so that
   companies and other industry representatives can see:
   • The challenge you worked on
   • How your team approached it
   • Your proposed solution
   • Your working process
   • The people behind the project
   Where appropriate, individual team members will also be linked to their LinkedIn
   profiles.
   This means that the hackathon is not only an exercise.
   It can become something you can show, share and talk about when approaching
   companies for LIA.
   What we expect from you
   Treat the company challenge as if it were a real professional assignment.
   We expect you to:
   • Participate actively in your team
   • Respect the different competencies within the team
   • Focus on the company's actual problem
   • Make decisions and move forward
   • Produce something concrete and understandable
   • Present professionally
   • Be curious and ambitious without making the solution unnecessarily
   complicated
   You will have limited time.
   That is intentional.
   The goal is not perfection.
   The goal is to demonstrate how you think, how you collaborate and what you can
   create together.
   Think beyond the assignment
   When you meet the company, do not only think:
   "What do we need to submit?"
   Think:
   "How can we make this company remember our team?"
   The hackathon is an opportunity to demonstrate the value of the skills you have been
   developing at Futuregames.
   Use it well.
   Key dates
   13 October | 09:00-10:30
   Kick-off, company cases and team start
   13-14 October
   Team sprint and production
   15 October | 10:00-12:00
   Final pitches to participating companies
   Final output
   A presentation in PDF format and a shareable digital result, published through GitHub,
   Vercel or another suitable platform
